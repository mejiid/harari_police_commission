# Product Vision

## Project Name
Prison Police Commission — Official Website

## Summary
A public-facing institutional website for the Prison Police Commission. The site serves as the official digital presence of the commission, providing the public with access to news, reports, and a way to contact the commission directly.

## Core Purpose
- Inform the public about the commission's activities, news, and published reports
- Provide downloadable official documents (PDFs)
- Allow visitors to contact the commission via a structured contact form

## Target Users
- General public seeking information about the Prison Police Commission
- Journalists and researchers accessing reports and news
- Commission staff managing content via the admin panel

## Key Goals
- Transparency: Make reports and news easily accessible to the public
- Accessibility: Site must be usable by all visitors
- Maintainability: Non-technical staff can manage content without developer involvement
- Trust: Professional, government-grade design and security

## Out of Scope (so far)
- Complaint submission portal
- Internal staff tools beyond CMS
- Inmate or case management systems
